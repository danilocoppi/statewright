Scenarios

Pays in time

Starts Mon 5 Jan 2026, 09:00

A cross-border send that is paid, converted and paid out in full before the offer expires.

  1. StartStarts in clear, running and awaiting_funds.
  2. 1payin.detectedThe payer's money is seen: the expiry clock stops.
  3. 2payin.confirmedThe pay-in is confirmed, and the money is converted.
  4. 3leg.completedThe conversion leg completes; the payout starts.
  5. 4payout.completedThe full amount reaches the recipient.
Verification pauses the clock

Starts Mon 5 Jan 2026, 09:00

While the payer verifies their identity, the expiry deadline stops counting.

  1. StartStarts in clear, running and awaiting_funds.
  2. 1verify.startedThe payer starts identity verification. The clock stops.
  3. 22 days laterTwo days pass; nothing is due while the clock is paused.
  4. 3verify.endedVerification ends; the clock runs again, the deadline extended.
