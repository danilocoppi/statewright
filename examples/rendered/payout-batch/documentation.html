A payout is refused

Starts Mon 5 Jan 2026, 09:00

Two payouts: the Pix goes through, the TED is refused by the bank and flagged for review. The batch is booked and announced, and waits for the bank's confirmation.

  1. StartWaits at the bank confirms. Raises batch.paid.
  2. 1bank.confirmedThe bank confirms the batch. One payout was refused, so the batch ends with something to review.
